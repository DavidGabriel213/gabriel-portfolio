About Me

Turning Data Into
Deployable Intelligence

I am a 400-level Mathematics undergraduate at Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi, with a CGPA of 4.76/5.00. I am a self-taught Machine Learning Engineer who began coding on an Android phone and has since built and deployed eight complete end-to-end ML projects as live web applications.

My work sits at the intersection of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence — applying the rigorous foundations of linear algebra, calculus, probability theory and statistics to real-world prediction and classification problems in Nigerian domains: healthcare, agriculture, education, finance and fraud detection.

My current research focuses on Explainable AI (XAI) using SHAP analysis for student dropout prediction in Nigerian universities — a project combining cooperative game theory, gradient boosting and Flask deployment.
